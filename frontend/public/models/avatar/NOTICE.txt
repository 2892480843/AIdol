Changes: adult shape and facial target combination; subdivision and shape baking;
53-bone game-engine skinning; fitted independent wardrobe layers; closed blazer
with builtin shirt/tie; opaque torso/pelvis coverage; clean low shoes; dark hair
PBR and alpha tuning; web texture optimization; relaxed arm authoring previews. Exported GLBs retain the binding pose;
the application runtime owns arm posture. Female rear hair is refitted around the
closed blazer without changing front/scalp. Covered body faces retain geometry and
normals in a conditional avatarHiddenByOuterwear material. Female trousers preserve
their original Basis and use the native OuterwearLayerFit morph only with outerwear.
