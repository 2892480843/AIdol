Original fictional adult reference-derived character generated with the project
account in Tripo Studio H3.1, 2026-10-18. Rose-pink knit, mint pleated skirt and
yellow accents. The portrait reference comes from the user's supplied image;
the outfit reference was edited with imagegen while preserving that character.

The web asset uses one coherent full-body reconstruction, preserving its original
surface geometry, UVs and normals. The separately reconstructed high-detail
portrait supplies a frontal facial base-color projection with a soft edge blend.
Separate portrait/head mesh splices were rejected because their hairline/neck
joins failed close-up visual inspection; those spliced meshes are not shipped.
All geometry is real three-dimensional mesh data, with embedded PBR textures.
Desktop uses up to 8K base color; compact devices use up to 4K base color.

Presentation: fixed-pose complete outfit, unrigged. Rotation, zoom and rendered
photo/video capture are supported. This asset does not provide articulated body
animation, cloth simulation or individual garment replacement. Single-image
reconstruction cannot guarantee exact identity or unseen back/side details.
