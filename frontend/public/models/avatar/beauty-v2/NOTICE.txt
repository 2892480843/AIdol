Original fictional adult female and male fashion characters, created from original
AI-authored reference images with Tripo Studio H3.1 on 2026-10-18 for the project
account. No private user photograph or supplied video was uploaded.

Generation: ultra geometry quality, 8K textures, PBR, delight.
Web geometry: approximately 150,000 triangles per character. These exports are
unrigged, fixed-pose complete outfits. Their source geometry, UVs and normals
are preserved byte-for-byte during web packaging.
Desktop base-color texture: 8192px; other maps preserved up to 4096px.
Compact base-color texture: 4096px; other maps preserved up to 2048px.
Metallic factor is zero and roughness is clamped to at least 174/255 to reduce
plastic-like highlights on knit, fabric and skin. Base color is not repainted.
Original high-poly and 8K GLBs are retained in the local verification archive.

These complete surfaces do not support independent garment replacement, skeletal
animation, or a personal photo-to-3D reconstruction. The separate sample mode
supports garment mixing. A high polygon count is not an aesthetic certification.
